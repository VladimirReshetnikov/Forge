\begin{abstract}
Forge already contains a broad collection of certificate languages and proof-planning ideas. Adding another scheduler, a generic sum-of-squares recommendation, or ordinary affine witness synthesis would mostly duplicate that work. This article develops a narrower but substantial missing lane: exact bivariate real quantification with algebraic, potentially discontinuous witnesses.

For a Boolean combination $F(x,y)$ of rational polynomial sign tests, the worker handles one quantifier for $y$ and optionally one for $x$. It constructs a finite sign atlas, including every exceptional parameter at which degrees drop or roots meet. Projection is justified by explicit B\'ezout identities rather than a trusted resultant routine. Algebraic exceptional fibres are checked with localized rational-function arithmetic at an isolated root; no irreducibility of the global projection polynomial is assumed. Complete root covers and sign tables determine existential and universal truth and provide root-index strategies.

The accompanying implementation passes 24 primary examples, 76 invertible affine reparameterizations, 540 corruption controls, and 42 unit/regression tests. Independent SymPy checks agree on 250 rational-polynomial root problems, 194 defined algebraic expressions with six zero-denominator rejections, 336 complete rational fibres, and all 25 base cells of five exact parameter-region examples. These are separate evidence units, not a pooled success rate. The checker replays saved certificates with Python site packages disabled. The remaining Lean work is identified explicitly: exact arithmetic and Sturm correctness, root continuation, the source-expression bridge, reflected checking, and strategy elaboration.
\end{abstract}
