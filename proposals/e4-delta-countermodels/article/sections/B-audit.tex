The audit focused on the merged design and the code directly relevant to the
proposed integration. It was not a line-by-line review of all nine frozen
upstream submissions or every Leant/Djex source file. An incomplete code-search
response was not treated as proof that a term or idea is absent from the
repository. The claim of increment is therefore grounded in the specific
baseline passages identified in Section~\ref{sec:delta}, not in a universal
claim of priority over all existing literature or files.

The neighboring repositories are evidence of actual interfaces and existing
behavioral-verification mechanisms. Their test counts are not added to this
package's counts, and their source text is not redistributed in the archive.

\subsection{Remaining acceptance obligations}
The prototype is sufficient to reproduce the mechanism, inspect its exact
proof certificates, and develop the next implementation. It is not sufficient
to claim that Forge now proves additional Lean theorems. That claim still
requires the pinned Lean compilation, denotation bridge, completed induction
composition, and representative source-goal evaluation specified in
Section~\ref{sec:engineering}. These are explicit next engineering gates,
not missing explanations concealed behind a test-pass count.
