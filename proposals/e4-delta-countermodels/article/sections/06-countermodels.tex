\section{Finite Kripke certificates for constructive diagnostics}
\label{sec:kripke}

\subsection{The question this worker actually answers}
The input is a sequent $\Gamma\vdash_{\ipc}G$ in a named intuitionistic
propositional object calculus. Its formulas contain atoms, $\bot$, conjunction,
disjunction, and implication. A successful negative result means:
\[
 \text{there is no derivation of }G\text{ from }\Gamma\text{ in that calculus}.
\]
It does \emph{not} mean that Lean can prove $\neg G$, that no Lean term of
$G$ exists, or that classical reasoning cannot prove $G$. It says nothing about
additional domain-specific hypotheses that were omitted from the object sequent.

This separation is indispensable for integration with Djex. Its formula
representation distinguishes ordinary atoms, opaque source-type atoms, and
dictionary atoms. The object checker can validate the propositional structure
once it is supplied, but it does not establish that erasing an arbitrary Lean
type or typeclass environment preserves all possible inhabitants
\cite{djex-formula}. The initial implementation should display an IPC
countermodel without promoting it to unrestricted source-level uninhabitance.

\subsection{The certificate and its forcing semantics}
A finite model consists of worlds $\{0,\ldots,n-1\}$, a root $w_0$, a partial
order $\le$, and one Boolean valuation per atom. The root must be least.
Atomic truth is persistent: if $w\le v$ and an atom is true at $w$, it is
true at $v$.

Forcing is defined recursively:
\begin{align*}
 w\Vdash P &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad P\text{ is true at }w,\\
 w\nVdash\bot,&\\
 w\Vdash A\land B &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad w\Vdash A\text{ and }w\Vdash B,\\
 w\Vdash A\lor B &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad w\Vdash A\text{ or }w\Vdash B,\\
 w\Vdash A\to B &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \forall v\ge w,\ v\Vdash A\Longrightarrow v\Vdash B.
\end{align*}
Checking implication at the current world only would reduce the semantics to
something essentially classical and would be wrong for this purpose.

The certificate is accepted exactly when the order and persistence conditions
hold, every assumption in $\Gamma$ is forced at the root, and $G$ is not forced
there. The checker recomputes forcing; any solver-provided table of formula
truth values is unnecessary and untrusted.

\begin{lemma}[Persistence]
If $w\le v$ and $w\Vdash A$, then $v\Vdash A$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on $A$. Atoms use valuation persistence, and $\bot$ is never forced.
Conjunction and disjunction use the induction hypotheses. For implication,
let $t\ge v$. Transitivity gives $t\ge w$, so the implication forced at $w$
applies to $t$ as required.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Countermodel soundness]\label{thm:kripke}
An accepted model for $\Gamma,G$ rules out a derivation
$\Gamma\vdash_{\ipc}G$ in intuitionistic natural deduction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Prove by induction on a derivation that every world forcing the derivation's
assumptions forces its conclusion. Assumptions, conjunction rules, and
 disjunction introductions are immediate from the forcing clauses. Disjunction
elimination uses the forced disjunct at the current world and the induction
hypothesis for its corresponding branch. Implication elimination applies the
forcing clause with $v=w$, using reflexivity. For implication introduction,
move to an arbitrary successor world satisfying its antecedent. Persistence
carries the undischarged assumptions there; the induction hypothesis for the
body establishes the consequent. Falsity elimination is vacuous because no
world forces $\bot$. These cases cover the natural-deduction rules. Thus any
derivation of $G$ from $\Gamma$ would force $G$ at the certificate's root,
contradicting the checked result.
\end{proof}

This is a mathematical proof of the certificate format. To obtain a Lean
receipt, connect the finite evaluator to an existing formalization's forcing
relation and apply its soundness theorem. Completeness is not needed to check
an individual countermodel. Existing Lean formalizations should be reused for
this metatheory rather than rewritten merely to attach a Forge name
\cite{trufas}.

\subsection{Why classical tautologies are essential negative tests}
For excluded middle $P\lor\neg P$, use two worlds $0<1$, with $P$ false at
world $0$ and true at world $1$. The root does not force $P$, and it does not
force $\neg P$ because a successor forces $P$. Hence it does not force their
disjunction.

The same chain refutes double-negation elimination
$\neg\neg P\to P$. At the root, every successor forcing $\neg P$ would have
to force $\bot$, but no successor forces $\neg P$; thus $\neg\neg P$ holds
while $P$ does not. Taking $Q$ false everywhere also refutes Peirce's formula
$((P\to Q)\to P)\to P$.

All three are classically valid. Consequently an interface that turns these
countermodels into proofs of the \emph{negated Lean formulas} is visibly
unsound. These examples are not ornamental: they enforce the worker's
semantic ceiling.

\subsection{A branching model catches what chains miss}
Let the root have two incomparable terminal successors. Make $P$ true only on
the left successor and $Q$ true only on the right. At the root neither
$P\to Q$ nor $Q\to P$ holds. Thus
\[
 (P\to Q)\lor(Q\to P)
\]
is refuted. With one successor making $P$ true and the other keeping it false,
the root also refutes $\neg P\lor\neg\neg P$.

The prototype finds both at three worlds. Restricting search to linear chains
would miss these examples regardless of its chain-length budget. The
implemented proposer enumerates rooted trees, not just chains; the checker
accepts arbitrary rooted finite partial orders, including a separately tested
diamond.

\subsection{The executable bounded proposer}
The proposer increases $n$ from one to a configured world bound. For each
rooted tree it enumerates atomic upward-closed truth sets, evaluates formulas
using bit masks, and proposes a model as soon as the root satisfies the context
but not the goal. Nodes are numbered with parents preceding children. This
may enumerate isomorphic trees more than once; no minimality or symmetry
optimization is claimed.

Replay is separate: it validates the explicit Boolean relation matrix and uses
a cached recursive forcing function that quantifies over all successors.
Randomly generated formulas are evaluated through both paths in the test
suite. Stored models are replayed without the proposer and without site
packages.

When the model budget or world bound is exhausted, the result is \unknown{}.
In particular, the eight familiar IPC-valid schemas in the corpus are not
``proved'' by absence of a bounded countermodel. They serve as positive controls
against an invalid refuter. The fact that IPC has suitable finite models does
not turn an arbitrary three-world cap into a decision procedure.

\subsection{A concrete scalable SAT backend, proposed but not executed}
For a fixed $n$, introduce relation variables $R_{ij}$, atomic valuation
variables $V_{i,p}$, and formula-value variables $T_{i,A}$ for each subformula.
Require reflexivity, transitivity, antisymmetry, a least root, and
\[
 R_{ij}\land V_{i,p}\Longrightarrow V_{j,p}.
\]
Constrain the Boolean connectives by biconditionals, and use
\[
 T_{i,A\to B}\ \longleftrightarrow\
 \bigwedge_{j<n}(\neg R_{ij}\lor\neg T_{j,A}\lor T_{j,B}).
\]
Finally assert every $T_{0,A}$ for $A\in\Gamma$ and $\neg T_{0,G}$. Standard
Tseitin variables turn the conjunctions into clauses without exponential
formula expansion. This encoding has $O(n^3)$ transitivity constraints and
$O(n^2s)$ implication work for $s$ subformulas.

A SAT library such as PySAT is a replaceable \emph{model proposer}; its
satisfying assignment is decoded into the existing certificate and independently
checked \cite{pysat}. An UNSAT answer for a fixed $n$ carries no unbounded
noninhabitation or validity authority in this design. That is true even when
the SAT solver itself is correct.
