# First Lean integration contract

1. Normalize a reflected two-state retry loop to an absorbing finite kernel.
   Prove every probability and stopping/cost equation in Lean. The certificate
   producer must not be permitted to replace the table or omit enabled actions.
2. Reconstruct the rational potential [2,0] and its unit-cost supersolution proof.
   Prove all finite-prefix cost bounds using the generic induction theorem.
3. Prove the nonnegative extended-expectation limit bridge. Only then state the
   expected hitting-time result on the original source loop.
4. Compile with the declared pin, retain the generated proof and actual axiom
   inventory, and rerun a false bound and altered-source control.
5. Add the success/failure MDP. Prove max reachability 1/2 but reject interpreting
   exit-to-success-or-failure rank as finite expected time to success.
6. Add exact joint witnesses and sequential Hall deletion replay. Keep no-strong-
   bisimulation, no-one-step-rate, and source-level behavior refutations separate.

Do not interpret a divergent loop as a total output PMF without a termination/mass
proof. Do not normalize subdistribution mass silently. Do not use unconditional
noise moments as a substitute for the source's conditional fresh-sampling law.
Do not give the expectation transformer the ring-homomorphism laws of deterministic
substitution. Do not prune a trace-equivalence synthesis candidate solely because
strong bisimulation failed.

These gates are proposed acceptance tests, not completed integrations. The included
Lean files cover only abstract finite iteration, rational fixed-kernel prefix
bounds, and one coupling marginal identity, and have NOT_RUN status here.
