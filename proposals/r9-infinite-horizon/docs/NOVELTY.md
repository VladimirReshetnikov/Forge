## New delivery here

1. Exact unbounded-stack pop summaries, productive DAGs versus closed exclusion
   relations, and a tested regular-target compiler with top-first suffix semantics.
2. Persistent adversarial strategies with distinct Büchi and co-Büchi certificates.
   Infinite execution is permitted and positive ranks reset after acceptance.
3. Properness-backed exact rational absorption values and closed-class refutations,
   with a finite-horizon error-bound interface for Lean formalization.
4. Worker-specific source bridges: suffix/context preservation, alternating
   ownership-aware strategy refinement, nondivergent stuttering, and probability
   pushforward equality. These specialize, rather than replace, the old boundary.
5. Executable, standard-library-only searches and separate replay plus extensive
   independent and exhaustive model-level tests.

The classical algorithms are attributed in the article. A finite-algebra or closure
principle could underpin the pushdown checker: this is not a claim that earlier
abstract machinery is incapable of expressing its local rules. The new capability
is the complete named domain and its checked semantic translation plan.
