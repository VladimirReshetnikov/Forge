{\Large\sffamily\bfseries\color{ink}FORGE / RESEARCH EXTENSION}\par
\vspace{24mm}
{\Huge\bfseries\color{ink}Uniform Real-Fiber\par Certificates}\par
\vspace{8mm}
{\Large Goal-directed Hermite forms for algebraic witnesses,\par exact solution counts, and parametric quantifier elimination\par}
\vspace{14mm}
\rule{\textwidth}{0.6pt}\par
\vspace{5mm}
\textbf{The proposed capability.} Turn a nonlinear algebraic existence or uniqueness goal into a finite circuit of polynomial signs. Keep the circuit valid when roots collide, eigenvalues vanish, or the real solution set becomes empty. Close one remaining real parameter by a complete sign-cell cover, and return exact algebraic witnesses when the supported concrete fragment permits it.\par
\vspace{7mm}
\textbf{Delivered evidence.} An executable SymPy producer; a separate, standard-library-only Python replay implementation; retained certificates, negative controls and test logs; proofs of the mathematical rules; and a concrete Lean integration plan.\par
\vspace{5mm}
\textbf{Trust boundary.} This is not an installed Lean tactic. The new checker is not formally verified. No new certificate in this package was checked by Lean's kernel, and no comparison with \code{grind} or another Lean tactic was run.\par
\vfill
