The main Forge evidence inspected was the README, `docs/STATUS.md`,
`docs/IDEAS-FROM-LEANT-DJEX.md`, the nonlinear arithmetic article section,
the merged provenance ledger, and `lean/Forge/Design/Contracts.lean`.
Leant and Djex were inspected through their READMEs, supported-fragment and
current-priority descriptions, and current branch records. This is a scoped
review of the merged design and its provenance, not a claim to have read every
line of all 27 archived Forge submissions.

Existing Forge capabilities treated as prerequisites include its certificate
boundary and planner, polynomial positivity search, fixed-interval Sturm
certificates, arithmetic witness workers, closure methods, mixed-strictness
polyhedral projection, and its later noncommutative, analytic and quantitative
workers. No claim is made to have invented or first added these mechanisms.

The new proposed capability is source-bound, uniform counting of distinct real
points in a finite nonlinear algebraic fiber under a Boolean sign condition,
with a specialization-safe monic tower and an exact characteristic-sign replay.
The added outer closure handles all boundary sections, rather than just generic
parameter regions. Concrete irrational root descriptors widen the witness
language beyond rational-expression templates.

The article attributes the Hermite and real-algebraic foundations to existing
literature. Novelty is relative to the reviewed Forge design, not priority over
the mathematical literature or over unrelated theorem provers.

The algebraic provider proposal does not claim to solve Leant/Djex's open
integer-indexing composition or simultaneous-universe synthesis problems.
