## Engineering novelty, not invented historical mathematics

Schreier-Sims, Burnside counting, Reynolds averaging, canonical representatives, and symmetry breaking are established methods. The contribution is the concrete interface and certificate suite, a complete local verification argument, separate executed checkers, and specific source-preserving integration into the inspected Forge program. The article cites GAP, SymPy, Mathlib, nauty/Traces, certified dominance/symmetry-breaking papers, and the symmetry/SOS literature.

## Distinct from existing finite quotients

The certified equivalence here is an orbit under an explicitly generated permutation group. It comes with exact group order, membership, coset coordinates, and transporters. It is not presented as a replacement for Forge's finite-algebra covers, reachable-span quotients, or probabilistic summaries. General semantic quotients are not claimed solved by a permutation action.
