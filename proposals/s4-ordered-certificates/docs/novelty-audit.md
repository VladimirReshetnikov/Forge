## Reused and deliberately not presented as new

The existing controller/worker architecture; trust/evidence boundaries;
source fingerprints; structural induction; arithmetic certificates; box
coverage trees in general; target-directed closure; immutable proof graphs;
finite counterexamples; polynomial ideals; observable spaces; finite-state
covers; pushdown summaries; quantitative probabilistic and parity-game work;
Leant/Djex approximation-polarity and exact-source acceptance discipline.

## Specific new deliverables

1. An exact unsafe-region worker for guarded nonnegative-affine natural
   resources, including reset/transfer/duplication, with a finite preimage
   clipping argument and independent integer-box replay.
2. An exact unsafe-region worker for unbounded lossy FIFO words, including
   multiple channels, with exact predecessor rules and explicit deletion replay.
3. Two-sided region certificates: immutable reachable-to-bad derivations plus
   complete original transition/basis predecessor coverage.
4. Global-antichain-aware closure separation, avoiding huge local Pareto bases
   using directly checkable global-region coverage trees.
5. A tested explicit-model implementation, full accepted model/certificate
   corpora, semantic corruption controls, and a precise Lean port/bridge plan.

Backward WQO coverability, Dickson's lemma, Higman's lemma and the standard
lossy predecessor operations are classical and are attributed in the article.
The direct-cover idea is presented as a concrete implementation/certificate
improvement, not as a claim of unprecedented algorithmic theory.

## Nearby capabilities that this does not claim to replace

A FIFO channel is not a pushdown stack. Infinite counters/queues are not an
exhaustive finite state cover. A minimal unsafe cone is not a polynomial zero
ideal. Universal finite safety is not a game-winning or almost-sure reachability
property. The construction does not solve the original Church indexing or
simultaneous-universe synthesis queries in Leant/Djex.
