\begin{abstract}
Forge already proposes induction planning, witness synthesis, commutative
polynomial certificates, finite-dimensional and ideal closures, and specialized
Ore-operator transport. This report adds a different mathematical worker:
reasoning in a free associative algebra while preserving multiplication order.
Its outputs are two-sided ideal receipts, finite rational matrix countermodels,
and separate operator-positive and trace-positive certificates.

The central exact result is a finite decision procedure for consequences of
homogeneous relations. A target of degree $D$ needs only relation contexts of
total degree at most $D$; failure yields a concrete matrix model of a truncated
quotient. A second exact fragment reduces homogeneous Hermitian-square search at
arbitrary even degree to a unique rational Gram matrix. Mixed-degree and
conditional positivity use bounded ray search modulo the equality space.
A structural involution/commutation graph proposes the rays used in the CHSH
worked example automatically.

The delivered Python implementation uses only the standard library. Its
independent replay program accepts 119 stored evidence objects, including 45
matrix countermodels; 57 unit tests pass and 195 deliberately invalid mutations
are rejected. These are object-language experiments, not Lean proofs or tactic
benchmarks. The accompanying Lean reconstruction targets were not compiled.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Delivery boundary.} No installed \code{forge} tactic, Lean reifier,
verified computational checker, or measured gain against \code{grind} is
claimed. The contribution is an implemented search/check layer with proved
mathematical contracts and an explicit path to kernel-checked integration.
