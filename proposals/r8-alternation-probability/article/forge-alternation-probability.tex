{\Large Alternating strategies, probabilistic couplings,\\[3pt] and quantitative liveness}\\[12pt]
{\large A repository-relative design with exact executable prototypes}\\[8pt]
15 September 2026

\vspace{5pt}
\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Forge already proposes a broad certificate-producing layer around Lean's
\grind{}, including induction, arithmetic witnesses, nonlinear certificates,
and finite closures. Adding those ideas again would not extend it. This article
instead develops four compatible workers for obligations in which choices are
adversarial, executions are infinite, or transitions are probabilistic:
parity-game strategy certificates; optimal finite couplings with Hall-dual
obstructions; alternating probabilistic simulation; and worst-case expected
termination with Bellman potentials and reachable nontermination traps.

The contribution is an integration design and a set of independently replayable
certificate formats, not a claim to have invented these mathematical subjects.
The article proves the local soundness arguments, states the exact finite
fragments for which the algorithms are complete, and identifies the source-to-Lean
bridges still required. The accompanying standard-library-only Python
implementation was executed against independent finite oracles: 884 parity
arenas, 400 transport problems, 180 Markov decision processes, and 100 simulation
problems. Fourteen semantic regressions pass, every deliberately invalid
mutation is rejected, and all stored certificates replay with search imports
forbidden. These are Python results, not Lean-kernel proofs or a benchmark
against existing tactics.
\end{abstract}
\important{\textbf{Reading the evidence.} The algorithms and Python checkers are
implemented and tested. The Lean lowering specimens are uncompiled. No new Lean
\forge{} tactic, source reifier, or formally verified certificate checker is
claimed. The proposed extension is relative to Forge commit
\texttt{c98e47c5f804e92880fc1d0e378c1b95832b685c}.}
