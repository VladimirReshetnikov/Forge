{\large A concrete extension to the Forge research program\par}
\vspace{11mm}
{\Huge\bfseries Forge Automatic Arithmetic\par}
\vspace{7mm}
{\LARGE Certificate-Producing Quantifier Elimination\\[2mm]and Canonical Witness Synthesis\\[2mm]over Unbounded Naturals\par}
\vspace{12mm}
{\large Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov\par}
{\large September 2026\par}
\vspace{12mm}
\begin{abstract}
Forge already proposes an unusually broad collection of proof-producing workers. This article adds a different capability: a compositional decision procedure for first-order formulas over natural numbers with linear arithmetic and selected binary-automatic predicates. Quantifiers range over all natural numbers, not a guessed bit width. The same compiler constructs regular graphs of least witnesses, including the next power of two and the next integer with a prescribed digit-parity relation. Its central engineering object is an exact certificate for existential projection: a locally checkable zero-tail closure combined with a subset-construction receipt. This prevents a subtle but fundamental error in which witnesses longer than their visible inputs disappear.

A standard-library-only Python producer, separately implemented checker, reproducible experiment suite, 90 stored construction certificates, and 643 concrete witness traces accompany the article. The mathematical foundations are classical; the contribution is a focused, executable extension to Forge with explicit semantics and a concrete Lean port plan. Neither the Python checker nor the compiler-to-semantics theorems have been verified in Lean. No new Lean tactic is delivered, and no performance comparison with existing Lean tactics is claimed.
\end{abstract}
